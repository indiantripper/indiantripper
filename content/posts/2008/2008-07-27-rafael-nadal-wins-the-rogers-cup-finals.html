--- title: Rafael Nadal Wins The Rogers Cup Finals date: '2008-07-27 20:07:22' id: '64' path: /2008/07/27/rafael-nadal-wins-the-rogers-cup-finals/ categories: - sports tags: - sports --- Rafael Nadal defeated Germany’s Nicolas Kiefer 6-3 6-2 in today’s Rogers Cup Finals at the Rexall Center in Toronto Canada. It is his fifth consecutive title and 29 consecutive win. Nadal is on his way to becoming world No. 1 . Congratulations, Rafael!
