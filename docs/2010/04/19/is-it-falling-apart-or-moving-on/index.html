Is it falling apart or moving on ????

H
HarshApril 19, 2010
3 min read

" I don't run from you , I walk away slowly , and it kills me cause you don't even care enough to stop me "

And this is what she texts me . I don't care ??? That is why I was waiting for her since last two years and so , without uttering a word , without asking for any explanation .

Please don't tell me you have feelings for me when you have no intention of doing anything about it when you know how much I care .

I can't always wait around for someone to fix my broken heart . I think , this time I have to repair it myself . They say " sometimes people make mistakes , and they don't mean to hurt you ".... but let me make it very clear ....  when they do it , they do it in the worst way they could .

You remember the smile and blush on my cheeks ??? Blame your eyes ....

Now you see this broken heart and this gloomy face ??? Blame your lies ....

I am convinced watching someone walk away knowing it might be the last time you ever see them , is some of the worst pain you could ever feel .

I tried my best , I gave you my all . But you let me down , you let me fall .

It would not have been so painful if it was just you who walked away . Letting go of you might not have been that hard , but letting go of your memories and what we had together is the thing that's killing me .

I wished I could take my heart and just turn it off . I wished I could give you my pain . No sweetheart , I don't want to hurt you . I just want to make you finally understand how much you hurt me .

I wish life came with a backspace . I wish life had a restart button .

You know what ??? You didn't break my heart . You shattered it , stepped on it and wiped it away . Its time to pick up the pieces , though they are not worth it .

I am still not sure how many times we will say goodbye before we finally let it go .

Why do you do this to me ??? I was finally moving on and you have to say things to make me fall for you again .

It was you who unlocked my heart and now I have lost the keys to lock you out . I miss you. You were the one my heart and soul confided in , the one I feel safest with , the one who knew just what to say to make me laugh again .

No matter how far away you are from me , you will always be in my heart ,I will miss you every moment each day , I will always love you . You are my first thought every morning , my last thought every night . But I wonder if I really ever mattered at all . The future is easy cause it don't exist but the past is painful cause its forever .

You made me cry , but I don't hate you for that . You made my numb , but I don't hate you for that . Its cause I care , but I can't hate you for that either .

You ruined my life . I wish I had never met you . I hate thinking about you but I can't help it . You hurt me .... You hurt me badddd ..!!!!

But do you know what the worst part is ?????????????????? .............. I STILL LOVE YOU .......
