First Day In college

By HarshJune 6, 20081 min read

what was my first reaction on seeing my college ?

contrary to my friends ...... i didnt curse it on our first meeting......it was a mixed feeling......neither too excited .. nor was i too depressed.....

Harsh Singh .... new inhabitant of B-308......I was alone in my room for few hours..... The terrified faces of my batchmates could be seen everywhere......One could easily spot a fresher just by the way he looks......Short hair accompanied by a full formal uniform in the absence of a belt.....Being two days late and going by the batchmate's reaction I knew that I was in for something ..... Only time will tell what seniors have in store for me......
