The Love Tag !!!

H
HarshJanuary 10, 2009
4 min read

Well its been raining tags in the new year and this time around I was tagged by Moi to complete this love tag …..

1) If your lover betrayed you what would your reaction be? 

If ??????? ....... lol ...... well what can my reaction be ??? .... It will take me few days to realize what has actually happened ..... and then the long journey begins ..... the long journey to come out of the shock , to stop consoling yourself that its just a bad dream and will end soon and to stop blaming the whole world for your misfortune ......and then just remind myself that

I don’t need you anymore, I don’t want to be ignored
I don’t need one more day of you wasting me away

2) If you could have one dream come true which one would it be?

Well if I could have one of my dream come true , I would pray for the Harry Potter world to come true and be a part of the Hogwarts school of witchcraft and wizardry as a desrving pureblood member of Slytherin House........ weird !!!

3) Is there any person, at whose face you wanted to throw your slippers/sandals/shoes at ?

When I was preparing for my entrance exams , I dearly wanted to do that with Arjun Singh , for formulating that reservation poilcy.... :x

4) What would you do with a billion dollars?

First of all , I would buy a Ferrari Enjo and go for a looooong drive ...... everything else could wait .....

5) Will you fall in love with your best friend?

Can't bet on it .... Can I ??? ..... 

6) Which is more blessed: loving someone or being loved by someone?

Hmmmm ....... I think the person will be truly a blessed one if he gets both sides of the coin going for him .....

7) How long would you wait for someone you love?

Can't say ..... probably forever .......

8 ) If the person you like finds someone better than you, what would you do?

If I love someone , I would surely convince her that for her , there is no one better than me ...... problem solved !!!

9) If you could root for one social cause which one would it be?

Child labour ......

10) What takes you down the fastest?

People who tries to interfere in every discussion and maintain a constant effort to prove to everyone that they know everything and are superior to everyone.....

11) Would you like to have the job you love or love the job you have ?

No second thoughts ..... I would like to have the job I love ..... loving the job I have is nothing more than a compromise ....

12) Darna mana hai... But what is the thing that you fear most?

The fear of being hacked .....

13) What kind of person(s) do you think the person who tagged you is/are(or the person's blog from where you have tagged yourself)

I don't know much about the person but her blog is very nice , well organised and can surely get you glued .....

14) Would you rather be single and rich or married and poor?

I would go for being rich .... coz if I am rich , I won't be single ;)

15) What is the first thing you do when you wake up?

I check for the inbox messages ...... can't afford friends getting  pissed off for an untimely reply .....

16) If you fall in love with two people simultaneously who would you pick?

That won't happen ..... Its not possible (atleast for me) to fall in love with two people simultaneously .....

17)Would you give all in a relationship?

Absolutely .... without a trace of doubt....

18)What's eating you now?

Well nothing , am not tensed at all ..... semesters went all fine and life has also come back on the right track now .... I am enjoying every bit of it .....

19)Do you prefer being single or in a relationship?

I prefer to be single .....
