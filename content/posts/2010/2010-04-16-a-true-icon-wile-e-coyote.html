--- title: A true icon >> Wile E. Coyote date: 2010-04-16 17:37:50 id: 3592 path: /2010/04/16/a-true-icon-wile-e-coyote/ categories: rambling tags: determination, failure, road-runner, success, warner-brothers, wile-e-coyote --- Frequently comes a situation in our life when we are completely frustrated , nothing appears to go our way , the blame for every other failure of ours is laid on God .... And finally we quit , thinking that the job was not meant for us and its better off doing something else . But there is a guy who stands completely aloof of this crowd , a guy who has been literally torpedoed a zillion number of times , but he still goes on and on , unruffled and focused as ever . The person I am talking about is none other than Warner Bros' Wile E. Coyote . He has been blown up , smashed , crushed , punctured , run over by a train , tipped off the mountains , thrown away in space , cut in half , set on fire , freezed to ice and has confronted countless dangers in his attempt to catch the Road Runner . But he failed , each and every single time . Most of us stop at the very first road block we hit and give up. But not Wile E. Coyote. When one of his plans doesn't work out as it should have been , he gets his focus together , doesn't lose hope and is back at his workdesk thinking about his next plan of attack. He always comes up with a completely out of box idea to meet his target . I think there lies a major lesson for all of us . If you hit a roadblock , don't waste much time evaluating why you hit the block .... Just collect your available options , devise a new strategy and move on. Learn from your mistakes and seek another tool to get what you want . This coyote guy never allowed the circumstances to conquer him . His focus was flawlessly set upon his target , The Road Runner ( whatever he meant when he said "BEEP BEEP" is still a mystery ) . He was mocked by everyone but with every failure of his , he was more determined in his efforts . Wile E. Coyote is a perfect icon for all of us who , at one moment or another , are troubled by our failure lack of success . Just let this guy set an example for yourself , and whenever you seem to stray from your path , think about Wile E. Coyote and all the adversaries he went through . That will surely strengthen your confidence and boost your determination . Cheers . :D
