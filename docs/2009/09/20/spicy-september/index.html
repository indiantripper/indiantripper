Spicy September

H
HarshSeptember 20, 2009
2 min read

The last three weeks or so in my life have been so much full of activities . This September has brought with it many changes . The academic session has effectively started no early than the first week of September , and thanks to the senior's reactions when they see us marching towards our class ,"What yaar , shame on you . You are in third year and still you are attending the class . When we were in third year , we didn't showed ourself for months ." , I am in no talking terms with the lectures .

This time around , freshers have been provided with a Z+ security , as if they are to be protected against the wild beasts , who are seeking even a tiniest of chance to bring their claws into action . The second years , who were going " Why ragging Why ragging " until last year have suddenly changed their stance and criticizing the new law against ragging . Who cares ? Now I am out of this moh-maaya termed "Ragging" . Few students will be caught and suspended and everything will continue like they have been until now . Rules are meant to be broken .

Not until this semester had I realized how easy is CSE branch . I never picked up my books until the 11th hour of the sessionals and I still completed the course with time to spare for more important things like sleeping , playing Counter Strike and bugging on net . Here goes my advice to all the guys and gals who are anti to studies : go with CS or IT , that way nothing in the blue hell can trouble you ( except Recession , ofcourse ) .

I have just made my way out of teens this year . The birthday bumps were not as horrible compared to last year , but still were good enough to trouble me for a week so . September 19th , my birthday coincided with the first day of Navratri . I enjoyed the day to the fullest . And right now , I am going to pack my bags . Yes I am going back home . Mass bunk until 4th of October . Appears as if Ravan desperately needs my company in his last minutes . Happy Navratri to everyone . Tadaaaaa .....
