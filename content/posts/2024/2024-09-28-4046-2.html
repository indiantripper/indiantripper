While I was migrating my blog to a new hosting, I was too lazy to import images, or maybe the size was too much for the plan I signed up for. I don't remember what exactly was the reason but now most of my posts have placeholders with nothing in it.

I feel like getting all philosophical about that, about how I can equate it to life where sometimes you are left with just placeholders, while the pictures are gone, but I will do that another time. Being philosophical all the time is not very healthy, neither is it fun. So let me start by adding some color to this space. Here are pictures of my rabbit and my aquatic inmates.

I have been contemplating migrating the blog back to wordpress.com but I am getting lazier by age, and there is also this feeling that I would want to re-migrate this back post some time, so I decided to let things be the way they are. Do not fix something which is not broken. Lesson learnt when I switched my job just because I had got my gratuity, only to come back a couple of months later. And time has flew by, I am very close to getting gratuity a second time as well.

It has been a long time since I have written this freely. Even when I write privately, I am very reluctant to write just for the sake of it. It has to be good - It has to impress - It has to live to a standard. But that just meant that I would stare at the screen, with fingers hovering over keyboard. I will try not to get bound by this anymore.

Here is another nice picture, from Adrai Jungle trail, where streams turned to river and we narrowly avoided getting our head count reduced.

Adrai Trek

It has been raining rabbits and fishes since last few days. Thunderstorm, a few days back, was the loudest I have heard in I do not know in how long. Jojo was scared to the core, or maybe he was just sleeping, I am not very sure about that - The rabbit does what he wants - We are just his caretakers afterall.

Our life is sum of choices we make. Trying to zero down on path that lay ahead, and find things which give me joy. Turned 35 last week, and in order to avoid being too philosophical at midnight, I am listening to Love Rap. Go and see this.

I guess I have already mentioned more than once that I am feeling very lazy, so not doing any grammar/spelling check. That's all folks.
