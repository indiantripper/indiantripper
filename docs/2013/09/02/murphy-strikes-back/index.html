Murphy Strikes Back

By HarshSeptember 2, 20135 min read

I am not exactly a morning person. I am the one who has this intense battle in his mantle every morning (of a Weekday of course), just to get out of the bed. Just to convince himself that it's all about those 5 minutes after waking up. And all the pain will be gone. You will feel like you are reborn. Everything will start looking so promising. And then you see the clock and realize that it's time to pack your bags. For office of course. No more college days for poor little Harsh now.

Having said that, don't mistake me for the person who hate Mondays. The long and eventful weekends that I have ensure that I welcome Monday the same way as I greet any other day. But as it happens with every good thing, someone somewhere is waiting desperately to screw up things for you. Something similar happened to me today.

Start of the day. Monday of course. And I knew that I have to visit Vodafone Store today. See when it comes to Vodafone, I know deep inside my heart that it's going to be a horrible experience. The only reason I haven't switched to other players yet is may be because I am too lazy, or may be because I believe that all others are also the same.

I have a history with Vodafone. They take 3-4 weeks to change my caller tune. All their customer care executives magically disappear when I call. When I pay their bill, it somehow makes sure that it gets auto reversed. It's just not working anymore between two of us. Same is the story with Dell. Two really bad relationships I have got myself into.

Coming back to the point. Second half of the day. I wanted to get the work done as quickly as possible and make sure to leave the store before apocalypse struck. So I borrowed my friend's bike. Even though I had my DL and all the papers intact, my brain said NO, but my heart pushed me forward. And like all the times, I went with the stupid guy. Reached Vodafone Store in some 15 minutes and saw a big "No Parking" board right in front of me. Still there were so many vehicles parked there that somehow I felt the board was trying to say "No Parking Space". I moved around for sometime trying to figure out some dedicated parking location, but to no avail. Finally, I parked the bike an a safe place near a Paanwaala's shop.

Time to enter the Vodafone Store. Mustering up all my courage, I stepped in the store, took my token no and started waiting for my turn. Meanwhile, I was also suspiciously keeping a watch on my parked bike. All safe. No worries. I was murmuring to myself. Eventually my turn came and I calmly explained to the executive that I needed to convert my Postpaid connection to Prepaid connection. He looked at me as if I just murdered someone. Then he left for a moment and came back with his supervisor. This was a lady of firm determination. And the look on her face says "So you think you can get it converted that easily? Come, let's have a debate". Followed 15 minutes of gruesome debate. On one hand she was trying here level best to convince me that this is going to be the worst decision of my life. And on the other hand, I was literally begging her to just get done with it and let me go. Time came for the moment of truth. She agreed. I was so glad that I won the battle. But then there came a smirk on her face. Her final weapon. The processing fee. I was instructed to go the cash counter, submit the fee and get my replacement SIM card.

Glad that finally it was finished, I paid the cash and was waiting for the SIM card when suddenly the madness struck. The guard shouted "Whose bike is this? It's getting towed". "Come on it can't be mine", I said to myself, "I parked it in the right place". Still to watch the spectacle, I peeked out of the door. News Flash!! Two bodybuilders were just carrying a Black Pulsar to the tow truck. I didn't know what got into me at that time. I started running towards it like my life was at stake. Right to the glass door. Bang. I didn't realize it was closed. Like a Bollywood hero, I didn't care for my wounds, got on my feet again and continued my sprint towards the heroine, the bike, who was being dragged away by the two villains. All this while, the vehicles safely parked in the No Parking area were just enjoying the spectacle. Cruel World I tell you.

Finally I confronted them before they loaded the bike. 200 bucks was what they said. I was in no position to negotiate. I was completely dumbfounded by the series of events and didn't know what to do. I gave them what they wanted and freed the captive. Follow Up. I asked them for the receipt. They gave me a big smile an invited me to the Aarey Police Station. I decided not to push my luck at this cruel juncture. I stepped back.

I came inside the Store, completed all the formalities, and left as quickly as possible. This city is no more safe for bikes. I was making my way back, trying to push the events out of my mind, when suddenly came a loud thud. What was it? Was it a plane? A bird? No. It was puncture. Say goodbye to next half an hour. Say hello to some bike dragging, cash outflow and a lot of sweat.

I finally came back. While I was narrating all the tragic incidents to my boss, my phone rung. My heart stopped. It was an unknown number. Could it be? I picked the phone.

"Hello"

"Hello. Am I talking to Mr. Harsh Singh?"

"Yes. Speaking"

"Sir, I am calling from Vodafone Store. We are really sorry to let you know that their is some technical issue with the SIM that you collected. It's not getting activated. Can you please come back today or tomorrow and collect a duplicate SIM"

I didn't say anything. All I could say was "Okay".
