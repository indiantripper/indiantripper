Landmark

H
HarshJuly 13, 2009
1 min read

This is my 100th post . Still I am unable to believe that I have come this far . Lack of patience in doing anything I get involved in has been one of my weak points and one of the reasons I felt that I would not go far with this blogging thing . But 100 posts is something unbelievable . More than one year of blogging . Moved from wordpress to my own domain . And now this landmark . This has been quite a journey so far and I am surely going to take it further . :D

Meanwhile the much awaited monsoons have arrive and though its not raining cats and dogs here , its enough rain to keep the heat away . Anyways , I prefer overcast to rain . Atleast you can go around your place rather than locked in the room staring at the surroundings from your window .

My calendar reads 14th of July . Do you know what that means ? That means in 3 days from now , I will be watching the most awaited "Harry Potter and The Half Blood Prince" .... I will surely come up with a review ( which will surely lure you into watching this one ;)   ) ....

And the very next day to HBP , I will be making my way back to Ghaziabad . Dean has been pretty regular in calling my number ( which happens to be my dad's number in college records :lol: ) . Well that reminds me that first year results has been declared today . It won't be much long until my results are declared too :( .
