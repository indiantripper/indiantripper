My life , right now , is marked by the incoming and outgoing of all types of forms , which I have to fill in order to ensure a security lest I fail to deliver the knockout punch ..... May be I did all those things out of sheer frustration ... Or may be this is what I have become ... Still unsure whether this is a "make" or "break" ....
