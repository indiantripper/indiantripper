Another year of excitement on the verge

H
HarshJuly 26, 2008
1 min read

Just came back from Ghaziabad …. 8 hours of journey …. College looked different from the last time I left …. may be because I was seeing it as a senior now …. yes … after 1 year of toil ,finally I am a senior now and It feels really good to be a senior …..

Just 3 days back I left for Ghaziabad with Abhishek , Vibhuti , Cena and Das to pay our fees for second year …. but against all our plans , we were hit by an empty boredom prersonified hostel accompanied by the usual chaos of a UPTU counseling ….. when we were unable to spot anything productive we decided to go for a movie to kill time …

BANG !!! icing on the cake …. Shahid and Vidya Balan did all they could do to give me a brain haemorrhage.............Kismat Konnection was a complete flop …. All the plans and money went to dust…. And all we were left with was a major headache ….

But my headache was a bit relaxed seeing few of the terrified juniors …. Same as we were one year back ….. 8 hours of our returning back proved to be a worthy time for us to recollect the last year …. Boy !! did we enjoyed …. surely we did ….. it was a year to remember and cherish …. Looking forward for a sequel this year……
