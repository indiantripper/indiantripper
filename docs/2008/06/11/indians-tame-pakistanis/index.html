Indians tame Pakistanis

By HarshJune 11, 20081 min read

What a match it was.....the resurgent men in Blue thrashed apart the Pakistanis in their biggest win against the arch rivals ..... The pitch ...... as expected in Bangladesh ..... disgusting track ..... nothing in it .... it was as slow as one of my yearmates Bunka running in 100 metre sprint ..... phew ..... And even on such a dead track the sachin dravid and gangulyless Indian batting line up filed up a mammooth 330 ..... icing on the cake .... pakistan 3 down for 26 and since then there was no looking back ..... India register a win by 140 runs ..... Indian team now looks so strong and formidable ......Viru for his blistering knock was awarded Man of The Match ..... his opening partnership with Gauti set the platform for others ..... their IPL form continues and hope that it does so for rest of the tournament .....
