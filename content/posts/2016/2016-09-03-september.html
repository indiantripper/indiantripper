--- title: September date: 2016-09-03 17:38:20 id: 2815 path: /2016/09/03/september/ categories: rambling tags: monsoons, mumbai --- It's still raining here. Monsoons have been with us for almost two months now. They will be leaving soon. But until then, the city will continue to be at it's most beautiful.  September is here. That part of the year when you take a pause and think - "Wow!! The year has almost ended. But it started only some time ago". That's the beauty of time. It runs as fast as a Gazelle when it wants to. Otherwise, it just drags along link a stoned snail.  But when you know that it's galloping away, you should make sure to hold on as tightly as you could and just enjoy the ride :D
