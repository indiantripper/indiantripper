Zee News : Confrontation in Mumbai .............. ( Thats the news I have been hearing since last 1 week or so )

NDTV India : Bipasha Basu sets stage on fire .. Watch Billo's new video .......... ( Wohooo ... That is what we were looking for )

Aaj Tak : Breaking News - I am very happy on receiving the "Chhupa Rustam" award - Virat Kohli ............ ( Glad to know that )

IBN 7 : Why are hollywood actors necessary in big budget Bollywood movies ? ................ ( Great Question .... May be they are necessary to make the budget "big" )

India TV : Special Report - Milk flowing out from a "Neem" tree ... What is there is the tree's stem ? ...................... ( Finally ..... finally they have stopped talking of Indian snakes )

News 24 : Sachin is a fan of Big B and vice versa ................. ( Awwwwww.....)
