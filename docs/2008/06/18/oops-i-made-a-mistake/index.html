Oops ! I made a mistake

By HarshJune 18, 20082 min read

It was last but one week of april ..... Third sessionals were goin on .... and as usual I was sitting in my home .... as I did for almost every month last two semesters .......

But what should I do ???.....Nothing to study.....all friends still in the college.....others busy for entrance exams ....... as usual I relied on my PC for engaging me .... suddenly read somewhere about something called adsense - earn money at home ..... sounds interesting .... but wait a minute ...... you need to have a blog or a site to use it ..... hmmm twist in the tale .... no problem ..... will make blog to implement adsense .... piece of cake.....

On which topic should I make a blog .... utter confusion .... all the thoughts and guess where I landed .... Orkut ...... and made my first blog then and there ..... worked 3 - 4 days on the same .....

At this point I made my first mistake ..... It appeared to me that more blogs will mean more money .... I didn't realize I was better paid off making 1 blog earning me 100$ than investing in 100 blogs making 1$ each..... before I realized this I was already into blogs on softwares , e-books , games , symbian phones and orkut .... too late ..... only one way out .... try and manage them somehow ...

Second mistake what I made in a span of few days ..... clicking on the ads sounded cool .... 70$ in three weeks .... felt like a millionare ..... oh yeah ! placed in first year itself ...... one nice sunday morning .... I opened my adsense account and TADAA ..... your account is removed for fraudelent acctivities .... good going mr. google ...... keep it up..... dreams shattered ..... and now here I am with a new account ..... with 1$ in a week ..... and with a reminder of a lesson I learnt in , may be nursery, that "HASTE MAKES WASTE"....
