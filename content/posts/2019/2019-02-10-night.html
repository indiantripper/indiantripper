--- title: Night date: 2019-02-10 21:46:01 id: 3473 path: /2019/02/10/night/ categories: poem-humor-fiction --- The silence of the night calls out my name. To what end are you headed to, it asks. I wish I could tell. I wish I had the answers. But it understands. It is that time of the night. It sits besides me, comfortably sharing the silence. I know it wants to talk to me. I knew it still had many questions. But it kept silent. It understands. It is that time of the night. Countless moments. All those times when I felt numb. It felt good. Being numb felt good. It still does. I am not complaining. I am thankful. To all of it. To the silent night. I wish I could let it know. But I don’t need to. It understands. It is that time of the night. The time is me. I am this time. I wish I could tell this to everyone. But will they understand? Do you understand?
