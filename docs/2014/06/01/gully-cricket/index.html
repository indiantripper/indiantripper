Gully Cricket - Not Available on App Store

H
HarshJune 1, 2014
4 min read

Back in the 90s, in the era of Pokemon and WWF, when there was no mobile to distract you, we used to have this game of ours. Our own version of Cricket. 5-7 in the evening (except Monday and Tuesday - It was dedicated to RAW and Smackdown) was all about Cricket. And were the rules different from the international rules. Well not exactly. But there were a few unspoken rules around the game which every child knew by heart. Here I am listing a few rules of our Gully Cricket.

The Tools

There are few things those are necessary for a game of cricket. Two of them is a bat and a ball. Stumps, as we have always maintained, is a total waste of money and space. But alternative? How do you decide if someone is out or not? Luckily in the place where we lived in Lucknow, there was always an under construction building and you just got to smuggle in a few bricks for constructing your own stumps. And there was always that one architect in the group who was summoned in every time someone was bowled.

The Homo Sapiens

Leadership is something many people are just born with, rather than learning over time. We always needed that one leader to take the initiative and call everyone for a game (which was not an easy task I must tell, amidst all the scare of disturbing someone's Mom's sleep). And once again, there was always that one guy of whose Dad everyone was scared of. But he had to be called - He was the owner of the bat, and everyone would be like - "Tu Jaa", "Nahi Tu Jaa Main Kal Gaya Tha". And even if he wouldn't come, we would be so daring to ask "Achcha Bhai Bat To De De". He never gave it though, it was the second most important thing to one after life.

Khanna Ki Ball

Cosco was too mainstream by that time. And it was well known that if you need a good game, it has to be a Khanna Ball. Cosco or Lion were good only for a terrace game. Our leader again came into the picture for collecting money and getting the work done.

Winners to Bat

The entire bilateral series took just one day. And winning the toss meant batting and a 100 m sprint to decide who will open the innings. Winners always bat first - Jo Jeet Wahi Sikandar.

The Vampire

Vampire - Umpire, whatever. The most shrewd guy in the batting team used to take over the role of Umpire. And about bias? Sometimes things escalated to such a level that at times everyone knew that the next ball would be a No Ball.

The Try Ball

You try everything once before getting hold of it, don't you ;) Why would cricket be any different. If a guy was dismissed the first ball, it had to be a try ball.

The Haunted House

There was always that one house around the park which was owned by a really cranky uncle. Inspired by the "Collector of Bones", this guy called himself the "Collector of Balls". If the ball goes to that house, it ain't coming back. And the guy who played that shot - "Saale tune maara hai, Jaa tu hi lekar aa".

Team Fall Back

If you break something - Run. Take the bat and ball if possible, and run. Run for your life before somebody spots you.

Numbering

If the strength is below 8, it got to be a singles game. Write the number, hide it with bat and let everyone take their choice and be ready for a surprise.

Naye Ghode ki Nayi Chaal

As the match used to progress, more and more guys would join in. Everyone was evenly distributed to an alternative team, one by one.

The Curious Case of the Lost Ball

We were not millionaires. We could not buy a new ball every other day. One of those matches, which extended late in the evening, the ball inadvertently took shelter in the bushes. And that entire day, and the next day as well was spent in searching for the lost soldier.

Light Appeal

6.45 in the evening. Very dim light. 11 runs required from last ball. LIGHT APPEAL. The batsman could not see the ball. Match Abandoned.

The Fight

A close run out. Umpire crossing the line. It was a six or four? Fights used to reach another level and the final call was taken by the guy who owned the bat - "Bat mera hai .. Main ghar jaa raha hu"

Batters First. Bowlers Second.

If you are batting first, you can't bowl first. As simple as that. Objections Overruled.

Lost in the memories, I want to go have a cricket match right away. Can you connect to it? If I have missed out on something, do add it to it brothers :D :D
