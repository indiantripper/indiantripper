--- title: The Proxy King date: 2008-06-10 18:04:00 id: 48 path: /2008/06/10/the-proxy-king/ categories: college-2, rambling, poem-humor-fiction tags: class, funny --- We were always least interested in attending any sort of class last semester......who would spoil his sweet dreams for the classes......yet hardly any student was detained for short attendance......The reason for that can be proudly related to our excellence in the subject "PROXY". Now in the class with so much proxies , there was bound to be a Proxy King ....... the three contenders for that spot included me , Ankur and Amogh ...... and the best place to get that was our mechanical class .... marking proxy was a piece of cake in that period ...... As the end of semester was approaching Ankur's record of 9 proxies stood intact ..... and I stood equal with Amogh at 7......and I was all set to break it .....It was mechanical class.....the scene was set......sir started calling out our rolls.......roll no 14 .....then 15 , 18 , 21 , 28 , 39 , 51 , 52 ........dreams Shattered......students fell short and at the end of semester I was only second best at 8 proxies ........ Someone mockingly remarked "Better luck next time mate ...... ".....I was not sure whether those golden days will come again......If only one more student was absent.........
