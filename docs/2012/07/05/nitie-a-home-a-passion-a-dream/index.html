NITIE : A Home, A Passion, A Dream

By HarshJuly 5, 20121 min read

It has been more than 1 year since I have come to this place ... The place I could safely call as my second home ... But the zeal and enthusiasm has only increased with time ... The desire never ceased to exist ... And the undying fire only made it more and more special for me ...

It took me only a few minutes to fell in love with this wonderful place, with the Life at NITIE ... And I knew at the outset that no one could be as passionate about NITIE as I am ... And then IMPACT happened ... And it just added on to everything ... Getting into it was like a dream come true ... I knew that now I had a license to do whatever I could for this place ...

Roles have changed now ... And as I sit on the other side of the table now and interview these guys who we are going to pass out baton to, I am assured of one thing ... It's all going to the very safe hands now ... NITIE has got what it deserved, in the form of this new batch ... And foremost of all ... I can proudly say that I have met so many people in the last few days who are even more passionate about this dream than I was ...

 

Let the synergy flow and the peaks be conquered ...
Let the fire burn and the domains be discovered ...
Let the desire rule and the horizons be defied ...
