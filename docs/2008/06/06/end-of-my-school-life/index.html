End Of my School Life

By HarshJune 6, 20081 min read

Now after all the toil for the whole year it was time for results ....... As I always say "Exams always end up in an excellent way.....Its only the results that get spoiled"......

Seeing the intermediate results , i was ecstatic since i did hit the 90 mark . But then came the entrance results and BAZOOKA ....... not selected in IIT....neither in BITS.....27K in AIEEE.....and 5K in UPTU......all the movies , matches and time spent online last year did payed off....God bless bollywood , cricket and orkut.....

I have always been a computer guy....and I spend loads of time on my notebook.....and have never been a hard working boy.....

Now , as the counselling date was approaching i could see my fate taking me towards that university whom I loathed all the year ..... UPTU ......
