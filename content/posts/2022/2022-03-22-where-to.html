--- title: Where to? date: '2022-03-22 05:49:04' id: '3968' path: /2022/03/22/where-to/ categories: - poem-humor-fiction ---

All you got to do is separate from the flesh,

Leave all substance behind,

And allow yourselves to become a conceptual point, a view, devoid of any mass.

With that accomplished, you can pass through any wall, leap over any abyss.
