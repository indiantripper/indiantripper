the IIT rumour

By HarshJune 6, 20081 min read

30th of May.....IIT results out......i was in the que for reserving my ticket.....phone rung......

Ajay asked me "What is your rank in IIT , pshycho?".....

i saw a train no......and replied,"2572"........

As soon as i entered the college , i could see everyone flying towards me for congratulating me........OMG.......my room partner is indeed very good in spreading rumours......it took me some time to convince everyone it was just a rumour......

My friend Pranay hit the charts this time around......379 in IIT......topper in AMU.....37 in UPTU and 749 in AIEEE........congrats to him.....
