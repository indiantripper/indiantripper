UPTU…..It has been one week since I have been waiting for my second semester results to be declared …..and still……no official date has been announced……though many days have been sprung in the uncertain arena owned by the rumours…..today was also scheduled to be one of those days…..but it wasn’t to be…..gosh….how long I have to wait…..

Its not like I have hit a jackpot in the semesters and expecting 99% ….not at all…..but I don’t like to be in such channel of uncertainty and to be informed by someone that you have a made a boo-boo in the exams…..Its good to be done with the results…..to bring a sense of relief within yourself……to know what have you done in exams….Rather, what exams have done with you….

No problem , the next date in the air is 2nd of July…..lets wait and watch….Hope they declare it before doomsday….
