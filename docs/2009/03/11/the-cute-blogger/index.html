The Cute Blogger

H
HarshMarch 11, 2009
3 min read

It always feel so nice to be on the receiving end of an award . And guess what !!! The Cute Blogger Award awarded to me by Moi has made my day . Thank you for presenting me with such a cute award :D .

Now its time for the winning speech and I am supposed to present forth ten few things about myself for receiving this award .

Disclaimer : I have not bothered to show any signs of modesty . Currently I am totally self centered and the following lines consist of nothing but all praise for me . :D . Here I go .

In my school days , any complaint launched against me always went in vain . Until the final year every teacher thought "Harsh is too innocent to do this" . You can fool some people all the time and all people for some time but you can not fool all the people all the time . At last my teachers realized that afterall I was not that innocent and bestowing me with a few punishments won't result in any natural disasters (they also took privilege to call my parents when I bunked the Comparative Exams).

It was a few years back when I was in fourth standard and had gone to my village in the summer holidays . The accident was initiated by my wicked cousin ( who thought I was irritating him :x ) when he locked me in the house and went to Verandah to take a nap . Since the house was just confined to the ground floor , I decided to use the tree inclined to the roof to crawl my way back to the ground to give my cousin a hell of a surprise , telling him who the boss was . But the fate decided something else for me . I don't exactly remember what happened next , I came down but not exactly as I planned . The jackfruit tree tossed me down in a hurry . Result ??? Six stitches on the forehead plus a bagful of scolding to my cousin for causing the cute kid so much of trouble . :)

I am nicknamed "Harry" among my batchmates . Even most of the girls call me by that name . In their view , my face cut resembles a lot like Harry Potter ( thanks to my spects ) . Expecto Patronum . :lol:

Last year I was tagged "Sweetest Boy Of The Class" by the girls of my section. :oops: :oops: . No surprises . My innocent face can deceive anyone .

That's all about me and my cuteness ;) . Passing on this awards to
