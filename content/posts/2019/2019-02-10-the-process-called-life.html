--- title: The process called Life date: '2019-02-10 21:48:50' id: '3477' path: /2019/02/10/the-process-called-life/ categories: - poem-humor-fiction form: poem --- “What is that one species you wish to be extinct?” “Humans” Answered the majority of the people I asked this question. Is there any other species which would wish themselves to be extinct? I don’t know. Jungle, where everything is about survival, this thought is not meant for them. They are meant for a much advanced and idle mind. A mind and a body which is well fed. You will not find poor people in this bracket. There priorities are different. Why do we hate ourselves so much? Will it be right in saying the people who wish humans to be extinct will volunteer for the cause by laying down their lives at first? I don’t think so. Though it might be true that majority will agree that they don’t have any problem being dead. They are just hesitant to go through the process.
