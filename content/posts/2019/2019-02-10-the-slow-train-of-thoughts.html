--- title: The Slow Train of Thoughts date: '2019-02-10 21:25:42' id: '3451' path: /2019/02/10/the-slow-train-of-thoughts/ categories: - poem-humor-fiction form: poem --- The slow train of thoughts. Refusing to speed up. Good and bad in its own way. The tale of a blurred future. And an even hazier past. Where am I right now? Where do I want to be? I do not know. And may be that is the essence behind everything.
