A "discriminating" policy

By HarshOctober 22, 20102 min read

This goes to prove that even IIMs are not perfect . Even they do err ... and in this case they surely did ..!!!

This is so called "IIMs new admission policy for higher class diversity" ... Damn it .. Just awarding 2.5 marks if you are from a non-engineering background can not be justified ... Not by far ... This is a clear cut case of discrimination and I believe this is a way of IIM-L people saying that "We don't want engineers ... We don't want all the perfect students ... We need a diversity ... So this time around we are giving preference to a bunch of less deserving students to make our environment a bit more of fun ... " ...

I mean how could you just give such an unfair advantage to someone from a particular background . If they think that CAT gives an unfair advantage to engineers .. then damn it ... change the bloody question pattern ... change the entire take in process ... instead of devising worthless tools like this ...

If you want people from different backgrounds ... then reserve seats for them ... at least make the picture clear ... at let us know that "Look ... these are the number of seats we have reserved for you "engineers" ... go fight for these to grab a place along with other guys who are enjoying a "much deserved" relaxation in marks " ... I can sense hypocrisy sprayed all over the controversial issue ...!!! ... May be IIM-L was bored of the normal routine intake and decided to play a game of dies for fun ...!!!

A valid bias can never be considered a valid criterion ... Just depriving engineers from those 2.5 marks just because they have been performing well in CAT .. year after year ... can not by justified even if they put forward some divine reason to defend their cause ....

Thumbs Down ..!!!
