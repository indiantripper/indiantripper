WANTED !!!

By HarshOctober 3, 20081 min read


Harsh Singh, possibly the most infamous engineering student ever to be held in NCR fortress, is still eluding capture, RKGIT management confirmed today.

"We are doing all we can to recapture Singh," said the Director of RKGIT, K.B.Naik, this morning, "and we beg the engineering community to remain calm."

Naik has been criticized by some members of the IEEE and CSI for informing the UPTU chancellor of the crisis.

"Well, really, I had to, don't you know," said an irritable Naik.

"Singh is mad. He's a danger to any studious fellow who crosses him, engineer or non-engineer. I have the Chancellor's assurance that he will not breathe a word of Singh's true identity to anyone. And let's face it-who'd believe him if he did?"

While Professors have been told that Singh is on a medical leave, the RKGIT management lives in fear of a mass bunk like that of twelve months ago, when Singh convinced everyone to bunk thirteen classes in a row.
