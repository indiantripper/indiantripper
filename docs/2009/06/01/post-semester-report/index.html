Post Semester Report

By HarshJune 1, 20091 min read

Its over . The hurricane has gone past and fourth semester has now came to an official end . Exams are finally over and they went fine , even though I was struck by Diarrhoea during the ordeal and DBMS question paper tried its best to give me a heart attack .

I am online today after five long long days . Thats the longest separation between me and the cyber world for some time now . A partial reason being I was moaning over my server going boo-boo . Some freaking "Bandwidth Exceeded" notice kept popping whenever I tried to open my site . But alls well that ends well . Its fine and running now , so am I .

The good news is that , I am back home . Back after some two and a half
months . And it was quite a journey this time around . No berth available in any train , not even in Tatkaal Quota , and I could not muster up the courage to enter a General compartment . So the only alternative available was via Bus . I would advise everyone of you not to enter an AC bus if you have not got a blanket to protect your bones . Or else you will enjoy the royalties of Laddakh and Switzerland in midnight and your shivering bones will curse you for not protecting them .

But no complaints . Fourth semester is over now . It went past in a ziffy and I am half an engineer now . :D .
