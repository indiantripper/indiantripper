First Semester

H
HarshJune 6, 2008
1 min read

As the days were rolling by we were jelling together as a perfect unit........the whole proxy unit was set up perfectly........all the bunks planned smoothly........copying of assignments from others was the order of day.........days flew off pretty quickly and first semester was staring down at us.....

Ah!...after all the threats people gave me about the unpredictable nature of UPTU.....i secures 78.4% in the sems........honestly.......I expected a bit more......but still I did breath a shy of relief.......after all its UPTU.....all BEST OF LUCKS did paid off......
