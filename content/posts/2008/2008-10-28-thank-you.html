This is the gist of the hour long speech by Jessie Owens (World Famous Athlete) on his first gold medal in the Olympics ......... Very Inspiring ......
To those of you who laughed at me , thank you Without you I wouldn't have cried . To those of you who just couldn't love me , thank you Without you I wouldn't have known real love . To those of you who hurted my feelings , thank you Without you I wouldn't have felt them . To those of you who left me lonely , thank you Without you I wouldn't have discovered myself . But it is to those of you who thought I couldn't do it It is you I thank the most . Because without you I wouldn't have tried .
Be good and do good .
