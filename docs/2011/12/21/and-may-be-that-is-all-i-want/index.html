दिल की बातें

And may be that is all I want

By HarshDecember 21, 20111 min read

Late in the night
I am all alone with my plight
My lappy gives me a strange look
As if it doesn't know who I am
My cellphone lie next to me
With a cold indifference of someone in glee
I wish for one of them to spring to life right now
May be I hope that it will keep me involved
And may be that is all I want

Late in the night
The Book of Economics mocks me and says
Don't give it a try you won't ever get me
Still I keep turning the pages
Not even trying to understand what it says
The reason behind which I am unsure of
May be I hope that it will keep me involved
And may be that is all I want

Late in the night
I can hear nocturnals whisper
Calling me out
To the place where they all prosper
I know it is a distraction
And will lead me to the hollowness of the void
But still I don't know why I let it has its way
May be I hope that it will keep me involved
And may be that is all I want

Late in the night
I refrain from taking the easy way out
Dreamworld is giving me a call
To a place which would take me really far
The world of uncocsiousness could put an end to this
A place where this boy will be released from the plight of his
But still I don't know why I want it to be with me
May be I hope that it will keep me involved
And may be that is all I want
