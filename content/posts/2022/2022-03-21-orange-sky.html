--- title: Orange Sky date: '2022-03-21 10:53:30' id: '3969' path: /2022/03/21/orange-sky/ categories: - poem-humor-fiction ---

A slow train of thoughts.

Refusing to speed up.

Good and bad in its own way.

Tale of a blurred future.

And an even hazier past.

Where am I right now?

Where do I want to be?

I do not know.

And may be that is the essence behind everything.
