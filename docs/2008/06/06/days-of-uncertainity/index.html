Days of uncertainity

H
HarshJune 6, 2008
1 min read

As the days were ticking by I was getting more and more confused whether to drop and prepare for another year.........Every person meeting me marked my mind with one or the other suggestion......

Deep inside me I knew that preparing for another year will demand complete dedication and that is the thing which I was sure I wont do , for sure........

And there were my friends......my best friend Sanchit.....not selected anywhere......but still , was the happiest of them lot.....he didnt even knew what course he has to opt for.......one of those happy go lucks........Pranay , my brightest friend came in extended list in IIT ...... he had started preparing for the next year's entrance .....and others.....everyone was in mid air......

And then came day for counselling......first glimpse of UPTU administration.......That day was indeed very very long......7 hours in the que.....then came the turn......hats off to UPTU......

Next day.........I got the allottment letter..........and guess what ..... the trade was not CS ..... it was EC.....and the college was RKGIT........whose name i heard for the first time two days back.......Ah!..life was indeed taking me somewhere i didnt knew the route of....
