Back to home

By HarshJune 7, 20081 min read

now after the end of first year i am back to my home , the city of nawabs , Lucknow ...... Really it feels so good here.....

I am back to my home and I have adapted to my old life style which includes going to bed at 5 and waking up after noon ...... not to forget my mom's threats all the way.......and also my parrot kakul's fruitless efforts to wake me up with all the screams he could produce......

nowadays , I have put a halt to orkutting and am completely into blogging.......made 5 blogs since my return.......

Holidays are on.......everyone is going for some or the other courses......where should i go???......is there any need to go anywhere???......i cant find any need......I find my home , from where I have learnt everything till now , the best place to learn ..........

Avast AV.....today it didnt allowed me to download a keylogger......end of my hacking dreams for the day......no problem....i used another trick to hack few accounts today......although a novice hacker at present.....but still.....i m a hacker and am proud to be one.......

Its 4:30 now.....and i think i should go to bed now.......good night.....
