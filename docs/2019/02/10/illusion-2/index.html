बेतरतीब ख्याल

Illusion

By HarshFebruary 10, 20191 min read

The illusion of control.
The grandeur of strength.
The blink of an eye.
Green turning to brown.

The good part.
The cynic in me telling it would be over soon.
I don’t know if I wish it to be an illusion.
Or a reality.
