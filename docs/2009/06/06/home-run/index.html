Home Run

H
HarshJune 6, 2009
1 min read

6th June 2008 . Last year , on this very day I decided to conduct an experiment . Bored of chatting and social networking , I decided to experiment with the thing called blogging . At that time , I didn't knew if I will be really committed to my blog , or will it be gone too , like all other things which fail to interest me after a couple of days .

I am more than surprised that its been one year and I am still dedicated to  blogging . So much so , that I have transferred my blog from wordpress.com to my own domain . And now I have all the bases covered . Its Home Run . One year in running :D

And thanks to everyone from blogosphere who has helped to make this journey so wonderful , everyone on my blogroll , my reader and all those who have visited my blog even once (except all the bots who look so keen to comment on each and every post of mine :lol:  ) ....

The thing is that I really liked each and every moment of this journey . Today when I reflect back to my posts , it feels really great to see all the memories bundled up at one place ... a place from everyone can sneek into my world .... my magical world .

HAPPY BIRTHDAY Magical Me
