Lockdown Day 390

H
HarshApril 2, 2021
2 min read
  • Sitting inside a mosquito net, you do not feel trapped. What difference does it make? Trapped inside a pandemic. Trapped inside a net. Potato. Potato.
  • You are scared to have a beer. Why? Because it might give you a sore throat. And then everyone will be very sure that it is a virus.
  • You are not sure if you should plan a trip one month, two month or three months down the line. No refunds, bro. It is the pandemic.
  • One of the many saviors is watching Brooklyn 99 on a loop.
  • Also, you have all the time in the world to read all the books you can get your hands across :D Remind me to put a review of the books I read in last month.
  • Star brawlers also helped me keep sanity. Or may be it helped me to be okay with my insanity. I said that just because it sounds cool. I am bored.
  • I started different work out challenges, ran 10 kms and cycled 15 kms every alternate day. Got bored of it too.
  • Now I am more interested in finding out who is winning the territorial Dog fights in my colony. And what exactly is the status of 'Dog Idol' where all of them decide to howl at a certain pitch - Late, very late in the night. But I am not complaining - Someone to give me company.
  • I moved my blog from Godaddy to Hostinger. And since I invested so much time on it, I thought I will rather post something on my blog - which I have conveniently abandoned for a long time now. [Note: Hostinger is super cheap - I was thinking of shutting down by blog, or move it to wordpress.com - What is the point in paying 6k if I am not going to write anything here]
  • I AM BORED. WHERE IS MY BOOK?
