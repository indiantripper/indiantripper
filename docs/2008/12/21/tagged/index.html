4 min read

4 wickets down …. 1 more to go ….. :D … wait a second…… I am not talking about another one of those cricket matches ….. I am talking about my semesters …. Just one more theory paper to go …. And has it not been for the nine days gap in between , I wouldn’t have come online in my hibernating days…. :P ….

And what did I encounter ??? …. A full inbox …. Scrapbook loaded ....a denied laptop..... and a tag by mystical …. Rules are as follows ….. you have to answer certain questions and for every answer do a google image search and pick a picture on the first page with minimal explanation......

1.The age you will be on your next birthday ………….. I will be finally out of the teens….yey…..no more a kiddo… :D

2.A place you’d like to travel to …………….. Ever since I saw MP3 , I

always wanted to visit the Eiffel ….. ;)

3.Your favorite Place ……. Undoubtedly , India Gate ….. just love the place …. Feels so great to be there …. The atmosphere is just brilliant and you can’t resist spending whole day sitting there and enjoying the view …..

4.Your favorite Food / Drink …….now that’s a very good question ….. no second thoughts ….. any dish related to chicken and just see me pouncing over it ….. yummiieee……. :D …. And about my favorite drink ….. give me anything and I will not hesitate sending it down my throat …..

5.Your favorite pet ……. As they say , Dog is man’s best friend …. So why to leave my best friend for someone else ….. I just looovvvve dogs ……

6.Your favorite color combination ……..Lets see….which combination is the most dazzling…..a clear cut symbol of the devil ….. BLACK and RED …… never mess with the combo…. :D ….

7.Your favorite piece of clothing ….. nothing in particular …. Anything that I wear becomes my favorite …. P.S. anytime , Black is my color …. Any piece of clothing … just that it should be black in color …. ;)

8.You all time favorite song …….. the song that I can go on listening for the whole day … again and again …. Without getting even a touch bored or sounding repetitive…..

But in the end

Hats off to LP …..

9.Your favorite TV show ….. Damn it….I don’t have time for such idiotic shows…. :x

10.Full name of your Significant other : SSSHHHHHHHHHHH……………. :P

11.The town in which you live in : Just love the town I live in …… It’s the city of nawabs , Lucknow ……. Feels so royal out here…..

12.Your screen name / nickname …… Isn’t it obvious …. Magicalharsh ….. but that’s just for my blog …. Otherwise its CYBER MAFIA everywhere else …. ;) ….. doesn’t that sound so cool …..

13.Your First Job …… Oops ! I am still unemployed :(

14.Your Dream Job ……. Never gave a thought to it ….. I think still there is some time left before I start thinking about my dream job ….. Right now I should think about something like “My dream Report Card” or something else on the similar line….

15.Bad Habit you have ……….. Excuse me….haven’t I told you that I am an ideal student with none of the bad habits whatsoever :P …… a clear role model to everyone …. And the best part of me…..I never lie…. ;)

16.Your worst fear ….. To wake up one day …… and to find myself unable to log into any of my accounts …. Getting hacked is my deadliest fear ….. surely I am suffering from Hackophobia…. :(

17.The one thing you would like to do before you die ……. Hmmmm…..there are a number of things that I would like to do ….. but can’t think of anything particular that I would really push for …… may be paragliding is something I will love to have an experience of …….an experience of seeing the world from a bird’s eye….

18. The first thing you’ll buy if you get $1,000,000 ………. Being a millionaire ….. wow ….. what if I become a millionaire !!!! …… the first thing that I would buy is a Ferrari Enjo ….. I just love the design …..

I hope I did it well enough ….. loved doing the tag …..did it in a hurry ofcourse ..... my exams are yet not over.... :( ..... passing it on to Sakhi , Meghna and Prarthana .......
