Orkut Addict

H
HarshJune 11, 2008
1 min read

June 12 , 2008 .....just wished Honey Sir on his birthday through Orkut ........ now I rarely open my orkut profile as I doesn't hold so much craze of orkutting as I held a few months back ....... I was a lot more than a normal orkuteer in my orkutting days ..... I could be easily categorized into Orkut Addicts who spend all their time on orkut exploring its different horizons and searching for all the possible ways to break into orkut security and to find loopholes in orkut ...... All the different flooders , fake login pages , album hacks , logout scripts always attracted my interests ...... testing different flooders helped me to cross the limit of orkut scraps (100000+ scraps)......Making a number of fake accounts helped me to win polls.......that was an achievement indeed...... I almost assumed the form of a Mafia .. I will surely agree that I wasted my time at orkut .....It is very much an addiction......... but on the other side I learned a lot from there too ..... The wish to post designer scraps encouraged me to learn html ....... the wish to hack other's account encouraged me to develop fake login pages and modify malicious javascripts to my use ..... orkutting is the tool which paved my way towards blogging and though I am not into orkutting that much now ..... still orkut is one of the integral part of my cyber world .....
