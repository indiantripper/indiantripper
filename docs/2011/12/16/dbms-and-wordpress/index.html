DBMS and Wordpress

By HarshDecember 16, 20111 min read

Well this has become a routine now. Every time there is a exam and even though I have hardly started studying, I somehow get myself involved in all kind of technical stuffs, especially having to do something with Wordpress. For a long time I was thinking about starting a separate section on my blog where I could just rant about anything that I wish to, mostly related to my mundane activities, away from the home page, somewhere in the seclusion. In short, I was looking for something on the lines of twitter, only with no 140 character limitation.

And guess what. I chose this time to do this particular thing, with DBMS exam to commence at 9:30 in the morning and whole syllabus standing by. And even after I am done with the plugin and configuration and all, I still buy some time out for blogging about the same. Somehow you get a push to do all kind of crazy stuff and complete all the backlogs only during the exams. Let us see what DBMS has in store for me, or rather us, tomorrow.
