--- title: Like a Pendulum date: '2014-04-22 00:25:29' id: '2185' path: /2014/04/22/like-a-pendulum/ categories: - poem-humor-fiction tags: - memories - poem --- Well this one goes back in August 2009. Stumbled upon it while going through my MS OneNote diary. Funny part is that I don't even remember penning it down. Had to google it to make sure that it's mine. For a system, We make and grind away; We nurse our rage Into madness And our thoughts we repress. Memoirs and memoirs, Mirrors and white moons, This can't end so soon. Like a pendulum, Efficient, ruthlessly, Chagrin and restless, Everything swings Betwixt extremes, All in hope of being free. An inner peace, serenades, Something keeps you ablaze. A stitch of fate, A shroud of haze, That light behind the overcast skies, Has a tint of that peaceful glaze. Where did you learn to let go?
