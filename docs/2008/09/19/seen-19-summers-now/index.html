कॉलेज के दिन

Seen 19 summers now !!!!

By HarshSeptember 19, 20083 min read

The clock was ticking fast ...... 12’o clock was approaching in a haste ..... and I was engulfed with a mixed feeling ...... approach of my birthday combined with all the beatings that I was supposed to be at the receiving end of..... the traditional way of celebrating one’s birthday in the hostel ......

With 15 minutes to go for 19/09 , I took shelter in the placement cell in order to attend the calls before being thrashed ...... Going by the noise coming from the hostel , the search for the victim had begun and I had only few minutes in my hand before being hunted down......

20 minutes past midnight ....... I decided to surrender ...... As soon as they saw me in the park , a group of desperate to-be engineers were all over me and before I knew what was happening , a number of shots were engineered all over me at all angles . Followed by all the beating , I was given an unceremonious bath right in the centre of our lobby , I can still feel a bit of surf excel in my head ...... The ceremony lasted for around half an hour ..... (Poor Azhar , nicknamed Tau , also received few blows.....as he always does .... no matter whose birthday it is) .....

After all the thrashing and beating , I returned to my normal self within an hour ...... then came the time for celebration ....... cutting the cake and a bit of wild dancing with cake all over our faces(Cena being our choreographer)..... we had a blast the whole night ......

Raat gayi baat gayi ...... when I woke in the morning , I was greeted with cramps all over my body ..... it was a feeing like someone was piercing needles all over my body ......

Where is the party tonight ??? ..... In WIMPY ...... rain was pouring down ..... but still we made it to the restaurant ...... whenever we go to any place in a group , the photo shoot session is a necessity ...... everyone wanted to be captured in the camera in every possible pose ......

Done with the food , wallet empty , belly filled , now stood a challenge before us ....... the challenge to get back to college .... Its very hard to get a medium of transport after 9 in Ghaziabad ( and they call it 10th most developing city in Asia ..... height of misconception) ....... Anyways we were lucky to find a bus back to college ..... the conductor’s behaviour , though , didn’t went well with us ...... no problem , we greeted him with unparliamentary words at the end of journey ........

Turned 19 today ..... enjoyed to the brim ...... Now I am just waiting for my official transfer from EC to CS ....... that will make me study something I am really interested in ......Its raining here .... and my whole body is aching ...... perfect atmosphere for a nap ..... Good Night ......
