ज़िंदगी के किस्से

The Magical Word

By HarshSeptember 7, 20121 min read

Love…is it just a word?
or a feeling impervious?

The mirror speaks to me…
says my face scintillates…..

My fragrance was never known to me..
until u discovered it…

You have brought me the sky..
and i see myself twinkling..
just like a star…

You have brought me ethereal smile..
which is not pseudo anymore..

You have brought me life..
and esoteric love…

Thank u for putting your ’soul’ in me…
mine is not lonely anymore..
