15 and Counting

By HarshJuly 5, 20091 min read

Hardly anyone gave Andy Roddick any chance going into this Wimbledon Final . Even I was quite certain Federer will get past him with ease . Thats a common verdict if its not a Federer vs Nadal final . But Andy proved everyone wrong . He was the better player all the way through until the end . Well one got to be lucky to win such type of marathon matches . Federer , unlike last year , turned out to be the lucky one this year .

Right from the first set itself , Roddick was serving rockets . Federer was able to break Andy's serve just once in the entire match , and that too the last game of the match , which proved to be decisive . Counting three years in a row , Wimbledon final has been a thriller .

I feel sorry for Roddick . As he said , he will be back to get his name among greats like Pete , Roger , Bjorn and Laver . And I truly hope he does . He has surely got his game up by a few notches . He is not the same Andy Roddick as the one two years ago . The way he played today , he was just magnificent .

And coming back to Federer . He is now the greatest Grand Slam player ever with 15 majors under his belt , surpassing great Pete Sampras . He jolted 50 aces to win the final 5-7 7-6 7-6 3-6 16-14 . With 6 Wimbledon titles under his belt , he is just one step away from equalling Sampras' record of 7 Wimbledon Titles . Great going Fedex . Great going Andy . The two Gladiators .
