Somethings never change !!!

H
HarshOctober 6, 2008
2 min read

Somethings never change ….. No matter how hard I try , I find myself unsuccessful in reverting from my old routine ……

going to sleep late at night , or should I say early in the morning ( i.e. 6 in the morning when Sun wakes up or when my mother wakes up , whichever comes first)……coming back from my nightmares (or should I say morningmares) around 2 in the afternoon …… only to come across with the same dialogue daily , on wishing everyone morning ;” It might be morning for you …. But its afternoon for us humans ! “ ……

I am just at the starting line , if not one lap behind , in terms of my syllabus ….. but still I have neglected it for something I got interested in of late , Python …… Don’t worry Don’t worry !!! I have not hidden a 12 feet long reptile under my parent’s nose , I have not got so much of pets to feed him …. When I said Python , I was referring to one of those programming languages called Python ……

Two days back while I was surfing aimlessly …. I came across a tute on “how to become a hacker”…..It said..

This, of course, is the fundamental hacking skill. If you don't know any computer languages, I recommend starting with Python. It is cleanly designed, well documented, and relatively kind to beginners. Despite being a good first language, it is not just a toy; it is very powerful and flexible and well suited for large projects.

Why not learn something productive inspite of downloading all those videos , softwares and trying to hack into orkut account of others …. Thus I developed a friendship with Python …….Pytthon 2.6 to be more precise……..

Clock is ticking by …. Its not much time until I have to make my way back to college…..With no reservation in hand , I am destined to one more of those trips via Gomti Express , “ Blessing for the students in NCR”……..But no worries …… as they say “Ab to aadat si hai humko aise jeene me”……

If everything goes as per the plan and earth remains all intact by the time I wake up , I am all set to go for a movie tomorrow “Kidnap !!!”……lets see how good it turns out to be ……
