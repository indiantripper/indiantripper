Truly Random

By HarshAugust 27, 20122 min read

I open my book shelf ... Only to find a pyramid of books falling down ... Falling with a thud right in front of me ... And all they gave me an year back is summoned from my subconscious mind ... in a flash of a moment ...

I look at my camera lying in a corner of the room ... deserted, unwanted and uncared for ... And just a couple of years back, it used to be "My Precious" ...

My old Nokia phone beeps continuously ... the irritating sound of an expected network message ... Sometimes back it used to be the loveliest sound in the world for me ...

Good old GTalk settles peacefully at the end of the taskbar ... Sleeping peacefully, not to be disturbed ... And there was a time I used to log into it even before FB and GMail ...

I open my Rediff Mailbox ... The Orkut Passwords of all my victims still lie there peacefully ... Time Stamp of 2008 ... Long Before Cyber Mafia retired ...

Nescafe remains ... It used to be an excuse to take a late night stroll ... Now it's only the last resort for hunger ...

Rains used to be so precious back then ... I just use to wait for them like crazy ... Now they kind of irritate me ... Overdose of anything is harmful they say ... They are damn right I say ...

People say You have changed ... In a spur of moment and under the pressure of a heavy though laden mind "A Statement" is passed ... Introspect ... Look into my eyes ... And tell me if you have stayed the same?

Heart plays games with you ... So does your mind ... Sometimes you are high ... Sometimes you are low ... But you can't complaint can you?? ... After all who wants to live a monotonous life?? ...

Be a Phoenix, Rise from your Ashes ... Don't expect a Dumbledore or a Potter to help you out of fire ... It's your problem ... And no one else is going to solve it for you ... Deal with it ... The Bottomline ...
