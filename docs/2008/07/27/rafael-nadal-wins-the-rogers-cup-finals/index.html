Rafael Nadal Wins The Rogers Cup Finals

By HarshJuly 27, 20081 min read

Rafael Nadal defeated Germany’s Nicolas Kiefer 6-3 6-2 in today’s Rogers Cup Finals at the Rexall Center in Toronto Canada. It is his fifth consecutive title and 29 consecutive win.

Nadal is on his way to becoming world No. 1 .

Congratulations, Rafael!
