Govinda Govinda Govinda Govinda Govindaaaa

H
HarshJune 11, 2008
2 min read

yesterday while chatting with abhishek ...... suddenly sprung a plan within us to go for a movie the next day ...... busted due to the last one week of hibernation I wasted no time to ring up ashutosh and the movie was scheduled the day after at PVR , saharaganj ............ I have never been a great fan of Bachchans and neither have I appreciated Ram Gopal Verma on any given day ....... still I decided to prefer Sarkar Raj to Indiana Jones ....dont know why....... I was late in reaching the venue and expectantly was greeted by cold stares from abhishek and ashutosh .... thanks a lot to city bus ....... Luckily we entered the theatre in time ...... Now I had my fingers crossed as I was praying for movie not to be a flop ..... well it was not .......picturising Nagres as the powerhouse of Maharashtra.....showing the politics prevelant at present......it was certainly not a flop.....but neither was it a hit .......... I still hold no idea what Aishwarya Rai was doing in the movie .... and Abhishek Bachchan ..... he just stared everyone the whole movie and repeated the same dialogue again and again "Main sambhaal loonga " ....... at last it was a relief when a sharp shooter came to our rescue buzzing off Abhishek ...... climax .... Amitabh killed all his enemies .... and guess who is the new sarkar ...... it was none other than Aishwarya Rai .... BAZOOKA ...... Well , at the end of it I was not as disappointed as Ashutosh was .... the reason being I started perceiving Sarkar as a comedy and thus extracted a lot of enjoyment out of it ..... just waiting to tell Nikhil about Abhishek's death .... he being his die hard fan ...... just loved two things in the movie .... one were the impactful dialogues of amitabh bachchan......for instance

"agar hum zamaane ke saath badal gaye .....to pata kaise chalega ki zamaana badla hai "

"kisi ka hatya karna jurm hota hai.......aur kisi ki sahi samay par hatya karna....Rajniti"

...........accompanied by the background echo of "Govinda Govinda Govinda Govinda Govindaaaa ".......
